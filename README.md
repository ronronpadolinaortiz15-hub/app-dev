# One Piece

> "People's dreams never end!" - Marshall D. Teach

A legendary anime and manga series following the adventures of Monkey D. Luffy and his pirate crew as they search for the ultimate treasure known as the One Piece.

## Main Characters
* **Monkey D. Luffy** - Captain of the Straw Hat Pirates who can stretch like rubber.
* **Roronoa Zoro** - Master swordsman aiming to become the world's greatest.
* **Nami** - Navigator and cartographer of the crew.
* **Sanji** - The crew's passionate cook and martial artist.

## Key Themes
1. Friendship and loyalty
2. Chasing your dreams against all odds
3. Freedom and adventure
